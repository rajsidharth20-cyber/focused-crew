# Focused crew

USAGE : it should be created for someone who uses to to take decision on what should he do next in organizing his schedule



FEATURE : it should have input of OBJECTIVE LIST for the day and also what other commitments does the person have for the day (classes, anywhere visit or anything where time goes), the AI will also do analysis based on OBJECTIVE LIST, COMMITMENTS and progress made to decide what should he do next



The AI will also ask the user what has je done in thst particular task and give suggestion based on the progress....USER must also upload weekly targets and AI will help to decide next work in that OBJECTIVE LIST



The objective list will contain subjects taken input as user and also his weekly and daily plan



The app will also summarise his progress of the day using AI and suggest any improvemebts
