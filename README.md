# Focused crew

FocusedCrew is a social study platform built for students who want to stay focused and motivated together.
Join study groups, start live focus sessions, see friends and fellow students studying in real time, track your progress, build streaks, and stay accountable.
What you can do with FocusedCrew:
⏱️ Start live study sessions
👥 Join and create study groups
🟢 See people studying live
📊 Track your study time and progress
🔥 Build study streaks
🏆 Compete on leaderboards
💬 Connect with other students
📚 Organize your subjects and study goals
🛡️ Join verified student communities

Stop studying alone. Find your crew and stay focused.
